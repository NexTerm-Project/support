# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: May 21, 2026**

## Overview

NexTerm ("the App") does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. Your privacy is fully respected.

## Data Collection

NexTerm does **not** collect:
- Personal information
- Usage analytics or telemetry
- Crash reports
- Device identifiers
- Location data

## Data Storage

All data is stored **locally on your device**:
- SSH connection profiles are stored in the app's local database
- Passwords and SSH key passphrases are stored in the macOS Keychain
- Session logs (if enabled) are saved to your local disk
- App preferences are stored in UserDefaults

No data is transmitted to any server, third party, or cloud service.

## Network Connections

NexTerm connects only to the SSH servers you explicitly configure. The App does not make any other network connections except for Apple's StoreKit services for subscription management.

## Third-Party Services

NexTerm does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Children's Privacy

NexTerm does not knowingly collect any information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the changes will be posted here with a revised date.
